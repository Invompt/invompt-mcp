# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We commit to a respectful, inclusive, and professional collaboration space. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

Use constructive technical feedback, respect differing viewpoints, protect sensitive information, and keep discussions focused on the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through GitHub private vulnerability reporting when the concern includes sensitive information, or through repository moderation otherwise. The Invompt maintainer role will review reports, protect confidentiality where possible, and may remove content or participation that violates this standard.
